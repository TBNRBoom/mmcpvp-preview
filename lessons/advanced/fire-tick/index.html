LESSON 08 / READING THE FIGHT

Fire Tick

Use the brief hurt window after fire or poison damage as a possible chance to hold ground.

2 min readMinecraft 1.8 PvP

How the damage window works.

At normal server speed, burning ticks every 1 second, Poison I every 1.25 seconds, and Poison II every 0.60 seconds. Direct fire or lava contact is separate.

A fresh accepted hit opens a half-second window. Inside it, compare new incoming damage with the strongest incoming damage already accepted in that window:

  • Equal or weaker: ignore the new damage.
  • Stronger: apply only the difference: new damage − previous strongest damage.

Neither case restarts the timer. After it ends, a fresh hit can deal full damage again.

Same attacks, different order.

Sword 7 → fire 1: the sword deals 7 HP. Fire is weaker, so that tick adds 0 HP, causes no armor wear, and starts no new window.

Fire 1 → sword 7: fire deals 1 HP, then the sword adds 7 − 1 = 6 HP. Total: 7 HP. These examples assume no armor; the comparison uses incoming damage before armor reduces health loss.

The stronger follow-up skips fresh base knockback, but sprint or enchanted knockback can still push you. Poison stops at half a heart.

IN A FIGHT

If already burning near an edge, a tick may help you hold ground. Keep steering; treat it as backup insurance.

TRY THIS IN GAME: Compare both damage orders with a friend on the same server.
