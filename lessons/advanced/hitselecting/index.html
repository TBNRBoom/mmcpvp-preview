LESSON 02 / AIM & TIMING

Hit Timing

Stop wasting chase speed between hits so you can keep your opponent in a combo.

2 min readMinecraft 1.8 PvP

Click for the next hit, not every moment.

Once you start a combo, the opponent’s knockback carries them away. You need to catch up before your next hit. Extra attacks can slow your forward movement even when they add no damage. Spam clicking through that gap can let them escape.

Keep moving between hits.

  1. Use a reliable clicking rhythm to get your opening hit.
  2. While they are being pushed away, ease off the extra clicks and keep chasing.
  3. Click again as they can take the next hit. Keep your aim on them.

Do not force slow clicks in a messy trade. The goal is a longer combo, not the lowest CPS. Not every swing causes the same slowdown.

IN A FIGHT

They keep slipping out after two hits.

After the first hit, stop filling the chase with extra attacks. Use that movement to stay close, then place the next click when it can deal damage.

TRY THIS IN GAME: Repeat the same opener with normal spam clicks and timed follow-ups. Compare how often you land the third hit, not just how fast you click.
