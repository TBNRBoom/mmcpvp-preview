W-tap timing

Build a combo. Briefly release W after a hit, then move back in as they fall.

Auto aim · W to move · Click to hit · E to rod · Space to jump

Hit at the edge of reach. Briefly release W, then press it again.

0% hit accuracy

Sword: F / J or click · Rod: E / I or right-click · R: restart / continue round. Each sword input sends three attempts, 50 ms apart.

Knockback and consistency of hit timing may vary from server to server.

YOUR GOAL
  1. Hold W. Aim is automatic; click when your partner enters reach.
  2. After a hit, let go of W briefly, then press it again.
  3. Keep the gap. Crowding them gives them a chance to hit back.

A warm-up, then the real thing.

These drills use a slightly wider 3.20-block practice reach, flight time, and both upward and backward knockback. Movement and jumps use Minecraft 1.8 reference ratios, with horizontal impulses scaled to 1.25× for these drills. Minecraft’s normal targeting limit is three blocks. Rod hits use Spigot-style behavior; server settings and connection delay can change the feel.
